How it works

Write the first stanza. The last word of each line becomes one of the six end words, and each gets its own color. The later stanzas and the envoi fill in on their own, with the required word pinned at the end of each line. Write the rest of those lines in any order.

You can also type the end word yourself, with punctuation. If a line ends with a variant (a plural, another tense, a homophone), it is marked variant. Accept it to count the line. For a pun the app can't spot, use End with “…” instead under the line you are writing.
